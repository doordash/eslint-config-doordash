# Support

## If you are looking for help with DoorDash

This repository is not the right place, and nobody who reads it can help you.

`@doordash/eslint-config-doordash` is an [ESLint](https://eslint.org)
configuration — a file of code-style rules that DoorDash engineers apply to
JavaScript source code. It has no connection to the DoorDash app, the Dasher
app, your account, your orders, your pay, or a background check. People who
watch this repository cannot look any of that up.

Please use DoorDash Support instead:

- **Anything to do with your account, an order, or the app** —
  <https://help.doordash.com>
- **Dashing, pay, the Dasher app, the email address on your account, or the
  status of a background check** — <https://help.doordash.com/dashers/s/>

A GitHub issue is also a *public* page. Please don't post personal details
here — an email address, a phone number, an address, or anything from a
background check. Take those to DoorDash Support, where they stay private.

## If you have a problem with this ESLint config

Open a [bug report](https://github.com/doordash/eslint-config-doordash/issues/new/choose).
Please include the rule involved, a small snippet that reproduces it, and the
versions of this package and of ESLint you are running.
